A place to think while reading

A useful reading surface gives the page room. You can pause beside a sentence, circle an idea, or connect two passages with a line.

The experiment is simple: open a PDF, read at your own pace, and leave your thinking on the page. Return later and find those marks where you left them.

In the proposed app, fingers scroll and zoom. The Pencil draws. The drawing tools are available when you need them.
