When can you cross each bridge once?

Imagine a city split by a river. You can cross from one bank to the other by several bridges. Can you walk through the city and use every bridge exactly once?

Every time you enter an intermediate landmass by one bridge, you must leave by another. The bridges at that landmass therefore come in pairs.

The start and finish are exceptions. You can leave the start without first entering it. You can enter the finish without leaving it. Those are the only places where a bridge can be left without a partner.

If the bridge network is connected, a walk of this kind exists when zero or two landmasses have an odd number of bridges. With two odd landmasses, one must be the start and the other the finish.
